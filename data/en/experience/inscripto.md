Inscripto is a multi-tenant sports event management and signup platform I built
and still run as its only person — product, architecture, engineering and
operations end to end — replacing paper signups and manual payments for karting
and tennis leagues. It has handled 109 events, 3,603 charges and more than
R$ 482,800 in online payments. The stack is Rails 8, PostgreSQL, Hotwire,
Tailwind and a PWA frontend, with Stripe, MercadoPago and PIX for payments, and
it uses the Gemini API to parse unstructured WhatsApp match results into live
leaderboards.
