services. I was there across three stints: full-stack developer in 2018,
senior developer from 2020, and tech lead from March 2022 until September
2026. I finished as the top all-time contributor to the core codebase, which
serves Disney, Warner Bros, Sony and NBCUniversal and accounts for 55% of
company revenue. I led the Webstores 2.0 replatforming and the company-wide
authentication migration to AWS Cognito.
