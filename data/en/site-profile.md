Eighteen years building Ruby on Rails systems, and still choosing Rails on
purpose. Until September 2026 I led engineering for a SaaS analytics platform
used by Disney, Warner Bros, Sony and NBCUniversal, where I finished as the top
all-time contributor to the core codebase. I am now open to lead and staff
engineering roles.

Most of my work looks like one of three things: making a slow system fast
enough that people stop working around it, breaking a monolith apart without
stopping the business, or being the person who decides what the architecture
is going to be and then living with that decision. I have taken Rails
applications from version 2 through version 8, which means I have migrated
most of the mistakes the framework has since fixed.

I also build and run my own product, on my own. Inscripto is a multi-tenant
sports event
platform that handles real signups and real payments for karting and tennis
leagues, which keeps me honest about the difference between a system that
demos well and a system that survives a Saturday morning.
