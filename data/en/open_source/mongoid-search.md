Full-text search for Mongoid, and the gem most Rails applications on MongoDB
reached for before Atlas Search existed. It has passed 720,000 downloads.

I eventually transferred it to the official Mongoid organisation, which is the
outcome I wanted: the gem outlived my own need for it and now sits where people
looking for it will actually find it.
